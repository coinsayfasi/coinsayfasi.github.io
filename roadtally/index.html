RoadTally

The mileage tracker that gets your tax deduction right. RoadTally automatically detects when you start and stop driving, records your route, and calculates the correct IRS, HMRC, or German mileage deduction — for cars, motorcycles, and bicycles.

Automatic, battery-friendly tracking

RoadTally uses motion sensors — not constant GPS polling — to detect driving, so trip logging barely touches your battery.

Three countries, one app

The only mileage tracker built for the US, UK, and Germany at once, with the correct legal rate applied automatically per trip and vehicle type.

Apple Watch & Live Activity

Classify a trip from your wrist, and watch your live mileage and estimated deduction on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and CarPlay dashboard.

Reports ready for your accountant

Export a CSV, a signed PDF report, or — for German users — a legally formatted Fahrtenbuch with odometer readings and business-partner details.

What RoadTally is not

RoadTally is a mileage tracking tool, not a tax advisor, and is not affiliated with the IRS, HMRC, or the German Bundesfinanzministerium. Always confirm figures with your own accountant before filing.

StreamFlow Player

A native iOS media player for your own IPTV playlists. Add your own M3U, M3U8, or Xtream Codes source and watch — nothing more.

In development · Coming to the App Store

What it is

StreamFlow Player does not provide, host, sell, or distribute any IPTV subscriptions, channels, or media content of any kind.

  • Xtream Codes API and M3U / M3U8 support
  • Native, embedded playback (no external app hand-off)
  • Favorites, search, and per-category browsing
  • EPG for supported providers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tabserve ("we", "us") handles information in connection with RoadTally: Mileage & Tax Log. Contact: teknopattv@gmail.com.

1. What RoadTally does

RoadTally is a mileage and expense tracking app for freelancers, sales professionals, and small business owners. It uses your device's motion sensors and location services to automatically detect when you start and stop driving, and to record the route and distance of each trip.

2. Data collection

RoadTally does not operate a server and does not transmit your data off your device. All trip data — routes, distances, addresses, vehicles, tax-region settings, and any manually entered notes — is stored only on your device. We do not see, collect, or have access to this data. Under Apple's own definition ("collect" means transmitting data off the device), RoadTally does not collect data from you.

3. Apple frameworks used on your device

Address lookups, driving-route calculations, and turn-by-turn directions are performed directly by Apple's own MapKit and CoreLocation frameworks, running on your device or against Apple's own mapping service — not a third-party service we operate. These are governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

4. Apple Watch

If RoadTally's Apple Watch companion is enabled in a future update, trip status and classification choices are transmitted directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, on-device — not routed through our servers.

5. Subscriptions

RoadTally Pro subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Your control over your data

You can delete any trip, vehicle, or all app data at any time directly within the app. Because nothing is stored on our servers, deleting data in the app fully removes it.

7. Children

RoadTally is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: teknopattv@gmail.com.
